Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects information that you voluntarily provide when using our service. This includes expense transactions, category names, amounts, dates, and any descriptions you add. We do not collect sensitive financial information such as bank account numbers, credit card details, or passwords.

We may also collect basic usage data such as login timestamps and session information to provide and improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for the purpose of providing you with expense tracking functionality. We use your information to display your expenses, generate summaries and reports, and help you understand your spending patterns.

We do not use your personal financial data for advertising purposes, and we do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your expense data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

You retain ownership of all data you enter into Spendly. You may export or delete your data at any time through your account settings.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal financial information with third parties. We may use trusted third-party service providers for hosting, analytics, or customer support, but these partners are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential.

We do not integrate with external banking or payment services at this time.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may also request a copy of all data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com. We are committed to addressing any concerns you may have about your privacy.
